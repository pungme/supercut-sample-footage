Tears of Steel - sample footage for Supercut
============================================

Tears of Steel (CC) Blender Foundation | mango.blender.org

These clips are cut from "Tears of Steel", the open movie by the Blender
Foundation (Project Mango, 2012), and are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 licence (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source: the 1080p release of the finished film,
https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/ToS/tears_of_steel_1080p.mov.zip
More about the film: https://mango.blender.org

Cut from the original film for Supercut's sample project.
Changes: trimmed. Each clip is one stretch of scene 12 ("the bridge"),
or one camera angle's moments of that scene joined in story order with half a
second of black between them; re-encoded to H.264 1920x800, 24 fps, AAC sound.
Nothing else in the picture or sound was changed.

Music in the film's soundtrack by Joram Letwory (www.tearsofsteel.org).

You may share and adapt these clips, also commercially, as long as you give
the credit above, link the licence, and say what was changed.
